# Libraria: questions for publishers

Use this in each meeting with a publishing house. Every answer maps to a setting or an open
decision in Libraria, so write them down as precisely as you can.

**Publisher:** ______________________  **Met with:** ______________________  **Date:** __________

---

## What Libraria does today, and what is planned (to explain at the start)

- **Sales channels:** today, readers buy on the website through Paddle. Planned: the desktop
  app (also Paddle) and the mobile apps (App Store and Google Play in-app purchases). The price
  will be the same on every channel.
- **Protection and reading:** the server already protects every book and ties it to the
  reader's devices: up to 5 devices used in the last 90 days, with licences that allow offline
  reading. The reading apps themselves are in development.
- **Publisher portal:** sales, refunds, VAT, fees, Libraria's commission and earnings, by day,
  book and country. Also CSV exports and monthly or yearly statements.
- **Rights ending:** when a title is archived, existing buyers keep it, but nobody new can buy
  it.
