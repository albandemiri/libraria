Discover A, richer

Same structure as A, with more depth. Each numbered change can be kept or dropped on its own. All of it stays within the guidelines: colour comes from the books and the palette, with no gradients or decorative graphics.

  1. Hero takes its colour from the featured book's cover. It changes weekly with the book. The description is set as a pull quote.
  2. New releases have a lead book, shown bigger with its description, beside a grid with genre labels.
  3. Most read uses large italic numerals in the secondary accent, with a stronger top rule.
  4. Genres become tiles with a fan of their covers, instead of a plain list.
  5. Publisher spotlight moves to a deep green band, with a monogram and a few facts.
  6. An editorial interlude: a quiet typographic line beside a "shelf" of essay covers, ahead of the footer.
